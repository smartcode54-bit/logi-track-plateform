PSAG1GO slice specification addendum: tenant API-key revocation

Scope: shared-docs/specs/mv-go/platform-support-access.md sections 4.3 and 4.4,
and PSAG1GO's tenant-bound API-key D2 work.

The base specification explicitly classifies revocation by the stored key owner,
but does not clearly decide whether revocation is exempt from D2 outside support.
PSAG1GO currently applies the same conservative restriction to tenant-key creation
and revocation: a platform human must leave support and the target tenant must
have no active tenant_admin membership or have support access enabled. Disabled
admin accounts still count. Allowed platform actions atomically audit and notify
the pre-operation admins. Repeated revocation of an already-revoked key is a no-op.

The review preserves this behavior; it does not introduce an incident-response
exemption or claim that the base specification explicitly requires this extension.
Any exemption needs a separate owner decision. Ordinary tenant members retain
their existing authorized key-management behavior regardless of support consent;
platform-owned keys retain their independent authorization.

Regression: TestGate1TenantAPIKeysRequireConsentAndNotifyAdmins verifies refusal
with consent OFF and an existing admin, success with consent ON, rollback of the
refused action, and exactly one notification across an idempotent revoke retry.
